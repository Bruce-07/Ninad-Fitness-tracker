🎯 My Weekly Rules

1. Weigh yourself in the morning under similar conditions.

2. Compare weekly average weights, not just one day.

3. If your weight doesn't increase for two weeks, add 100–150 kcal daily.

4. If you gain weight too quickly for multiple weeks, consider reducing 100–150 kcal.

5. Train consistently and aim for 7–9 hours of sleep.

6. Aim for approximately 1.6–2.2 g protein per kg of body weight daily.

2400 kcal is a starting estimate, not a measured requirement. Adjust based on your actual progress.
